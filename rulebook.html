04

START & RACE FLOW

Start position

  • Both athletes wait standing in the designated starting area at the opposite end from the rowing machine.
  • At the start signal, one athlete begins Station A; the other moves to the rowing machine to begin Station B.

Designated positions & direction

  • Perform all movements in their designated areas.
  • Dumbbell movements must be performed in the designated dumbbell area at the opposite end from the rowing machine. Box movements and rowing must also stay at their designated positions.
  • Face the box for every dumbbell movement. A rep performed with your back to the box is a No Rep.

Athlete changes

  • Athletes may switch at any time, with no limit on the number of changes.
  • A change is valid only after a hand tag between the two athletes.
  • There is no central Tag Zone. Tag wherever needed during the race.
  • Only one athlete may work at each station at a time.

After Station A is complete

  • After all four rounds of A are complete, that athlete may join Station B.
  • Both athletes may then alternate rowing after a hand tag until 8:00. Only one athlete rows at a time.
  • Station B is a maximum-distance workout; it does not finish before the time cap.
  • The rowing machine damper setting is unrestricted.
05

FINISH & SCORING

STATION A · FOR TIME

  • Record the completion time for four rounds: 168 reps.
  • If unfinished at 8:00, record completed rounds plus extra reps. Example: 3 rounds + 12 reps = 138 reps.
  • Finishers rank first, from fastest to slowest. Unfinished teams follow, ranked by most completed reps.

STATION B · MAX METERS

  • Record total rowing distance at 8:00.
  • More meters earns a better rank.

FINAL STANDING

  • Rank A and B separately within each division and category.
  • Each station’s rank equals its points: 1st = 1 point, 2nd = 2 points, 3rd = 3 points, and so on.
  • Add A points and B points. The lowest total wins.
  • If total points tie, the better Station A rank wins.
  • Equal station records share a rank: 1, 1, 3.
  • If both total points and A rank tie, the overall rank is shared.

A 1st + B 3rd = 4 points. A 2nd + B 2nd = 4 points. The first team places higher because its A rank is better.

A joint finish-line crossing or button touch is not used as the final score.

06

MOVEMENT STANDARDS

COMMON RULE · DUMBBELL

  • Use two dumbbells for every dumbbell movement.
  • Stay in the designated dumbbell area and face the box. Reps performed with your back to the box are No Reps.
  • Dumbbell drops are prohibited. Each drop deducts one rep from the credited rep count.
01 · DUAL DB SHOULDER TO OVERHEAD
  • Start every rep with both dumbbells touching their respective shoulders.
  • Strict Press, Push Press, Push Jerk and Split Jerk are allowed.
  • A rep counts when both dumbbells are controlled overhead with elbows, knees and hips fully extended.
  • If one dumbbell reaches overhead first, both must still reach full overhead lockout.
  • For a Split Jerk, bring the feet back together into a parallel standing position to finish.
02 · BOX JUMP OVER
  • Jump onto the box with a two-foot takeoff. Both feet must contact the top.
  • Full hip and knee extension on the box is not required.
  • Jump or step down on the opposite side. The rep ends when both feet touch the opposite floor.
NO REP
  • Stepping up onto the box.
  • Failing to place both feet on top of the box.
  • Returning to the same side.
  • Jumping entirely over the box without touching its top.
03 · DUAL DB HANG SNATCH
  • Lift the dumbbells off the floor before beginning. Start every rep with both dumbbells in a hang position above the knees.
  • Move both dumbbells simultaneously overhead in one continuous motion.
  • Power Snatch and Muscle Snatch are allowed.
  • A rep counts at overhead lockout with both elbows, knees and hips fully extended.
NO REP
  • Receiving the dumbbells at the shoulders, then pressing or jerking them overhead.
  • Starting a rep below the knees.
  • Incomplete overhead lockout or knee/hip extension.
04 · BURPEE BOX JUMP OVER
  • Face the box during the burpee. Chest and thighs must touch the floor.
  • Jump onto the box with a two-foot takeoff. Both feet must contact its top.
  • Full hip and knee extension on the box is not required.
  • Jump or step down on the opposite side. The rep ends when both feet touch the opposite floor.
NO REP
  • Chest or thighs not touching the floor during the burpee.
  • Stepping up onto the box.
  • Failing to place both feet on top of the box.
  • Returning to the same side.
  • Jumping entirely over the box without touching its top.
05 · DUAL DB THRUSTER
  • The first rep may begin with a Squat Clean.
  • At the bottom, the hip crease must be below the top of the knees.
  • Rise from the squat and move both dumbbells overhead in one continuous motion.
  • A rep counts at overhead lockout with both elbows, knees and hips fully extended.
NO REP
  • Insufficient squat depth.
  • Re-dipping the knees to finish with a jerk after the squat.
  • Incomplete overhead lockout or knee/hip extension.
