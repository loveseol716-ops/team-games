WORKOUT B

ROW · MAX METERS

Accumulate the greatest rowing distance. Maximum meters replaces the previous fixed-distance targets for every category.

The rowing window and post-A transition details will be confirmed in the rulebook.

One athlete starts on A; the other starts on the row. Tag and swap at any time.

SCORE
